## Track: Leadership

### Strategy Re-organization for a Global Volunteer Program

**Client:** United States Peace Corps | **Year:** 2024
**Tags:** #Leadership #Change Management #Data-Driven Strategy #Operations

* **Problem:** The Peace Corps Response program was siloed and stagnant, never surpassing 5% of total volunteer headcount in 17 years. A liquidity crunch and leadership transition threatened to stall integration indefinitely.
* **Methodology:** Conducted rapid process mapping (72-hour sprint) and stakeholder analysis via structured interviews. Formulated a "Remove the Crutch" strategy by setting a definitive expiration date for the independent unit to force full integration.
* **Outcome:** Successfully integrated the program into mainstream operations within 12 months. During the high-friction transition, the program doubled its volunteer delivery numbers, hitting 10% of the annual headcount for the first time in its history.

### Data-Driven Portfolio Management: The Peace Corps Country Portfolio Review

**Client:** Peace Corps (Office of the Director, Office of Global Operations) | **Year:** 2025
**Tags:** #Portfolio Management #Data Analytics #Performance Measurement #Strategic Planning

* **Problem:** Leadership lacked a standardized framework to evaluate the performance of ~60 country Posts, making resource allocation (growth vs. reduction) reliant on anecdotal evidence.
* **Methodology:** Developed a Country Portfolio Review (CPR) tool aggregating ~50 indicators across five categories (Country Partnership, Need, Safety, Cost, and Management). Built a composite indexing model and weighted scoring system to rank Posts.
* **Outcome:** Established the first repeatable tool for comprehensive portfolio assessment, equipping senior leadership with an evidence-based framework for strategic realignment and resource allocation.

### Peace Corps Post Recruitability Index

**Client:** Peace Corps Office of Global Operations | **Year:** 2024
**Tags:** #Quantitative Analysis #Index Development #Cohort Analysis #Data Visualization

* **Problem:** The agency lacked a data-driven way to diagnose why certain country Posts were "hard to fill," leading to inefficient resource allocation based on anecdotal complaints.
* **Methodology:** Conducted a comparative cohort analysis of the applicant lifecycle using Python (pandas). Developed a composite Recruitability Index by standardizing metrics (Acceptance Rate, EOD Yield, etc.) into Z-scores to adjust for selection biases.
* **Outcome:** Provided a diagnostic tool that identifies specific bottlenecks (e.g., initial interest vs. post-acceptance attrition), shifting the agency from generic solutions to targeted interventions.

## Track: Global Development

### Rigorous Impact Evaluation of School Rehabilitation in Georgia

**Client:** MCC & Government of Georgia | **Year:** 2014
**Tags:** #Impact Evaluation #RCT #Education Infrastructure #Georgia

* **Problem:** Post-Soviet underinvestment left Georgia's schools in severe disrepair, negatively affecting student health and attendance.
* **Methodology:** Pivoted from a Regression Discontinuity Design to a more powerful school-level Randomized Controlled Trial (RCT). Collected five years of longitudinal data, including infrastructure assessments, air quality measurements, and student learning tests.
* **Outcome:** Confirmed dramatic improvements in physical conditions and classroom air quality. While student test scores showed no immediate overall impact (likely due to COVID-19 interruptions), long-term data (3-5 years) suggested positive emerging learning gains.

### Structuring a Social Impact Bond for Youth Employability

**Client:** Millennium Challenge Account-Morocco | **Year:** 2018
**Tags:** #Innovative Finance #Results-Based Financing #Labor Markets

* **Problem:** High youth unemployment in Morocco required a shift from funding training *activities* to paying for verified employment *outcomes*.
* **Methodology:** Designed MCC’s first major Results-Based Financing (RBF) instrument. Defined verifiable outcome metrics and payment triggers, and authored the investment memorandum for the Social Impact Bond (SIB).
* **Outcome:** Established a new paradigm for workforce development funding, linking training provider payments to successful job placement and shifting financial risk away from the public sector.

### Synthesizing Evidence and Shaping Strategy for Workforce Development at MCC

**Client:** Millennium Challenge Corporation (MCC) | **Year:** 2020
**Tags:** #Workforce Development #TVET #Impact Evaluation Synthesis #Strategy

* **Problem:** Over $148M in TVET investments were building facilities but failing to improve job placements or income. The agency needed a rigorous diagnostic to understand why and reshape its strategy.
* **Methodology:** Systematically synthesized eight independent evaluations and applied the World Bank's accountability framework to diagnose "short route" failures between employers and providers. Co-authored the flagship "Principles into Practice" paper to function as both an evaluation synthesis and a forward-looking strategy.
* **Outcome:** Became the agency's defining strategic document for the sector, guiding over $190M in subsequent investments. It shifted the operational focus toward employer-led governance and results-based financing models.
